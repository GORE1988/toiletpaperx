# TOILET PaperX V25 — Performance Cards

- PICK YOUR FLUSHER replaces PICK YOUR FIGHTER.
- Ten performance-based card backgrounds, with arcade visual effects and color themes, in cards/performance-01.jpg through -10.jpg. These are themed variations of the supplied original illustration, not ten separately illustrated characters.
- Performance tiers: <-50%, -50 to -25%, -25 to -10%, -10 to 0%, 0 to 5%, 5 to 15%, 15 to 35%, 35 to 75%, 75 to 150%, >=150%.
- Card score, equity, position counts, tier label and date are generated from browser paper-trading state.
- Arcade sound peak increased from 0.075 to 0.125. Sound remains enabled by default but browsers require a user gesture before playing.
- Launch from toilet-paperx folder: node server.js.
- X share opens a prepared post; attach the downloaded PNG manually.
